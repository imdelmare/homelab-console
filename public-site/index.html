05 / Trust model

Small, imperfect, safely constrained.

Useful local models do not need direct infrastructure authority. Safety comes from the control plane around them.

01No generic remote execution

No arbitrary shell, SSH, caller-selected URL, or raw provider forwarding.

02Single-use approvals

Infrastructure writes are narrow, exact, input-bound, and decided by the operator.

03Central redaction

Sensitive data is stripped before observations leave the execution core.

04Structured evidence

Invocations, outcomes, findings, and task history remain reviewable after the chat ends.
